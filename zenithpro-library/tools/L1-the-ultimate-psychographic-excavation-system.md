PHASE 3: THE HIDDEN HISTORY PATTERNS
This is where it gets interesting. Dig for:
The Suppressed Solution Story:
Like how Ignaz Semmelweis discovered handwashing prevented deaths in 1847 but doctors rejected it and let him die in an asylum because they were offended at the suggestion their hands were dirty. What's the equivalent in their market?
The Lost Wisdom Pattern:
Example: Indigenous peoples used willow bark for pain for centuries (aspirin's origin), but it was dismissed as "primitive medicine" until Bayer could profit from it. What ancestral/traditional solution in their space was dismissed then "rediscovered"?
The Profitable Suppression:
Like how the sugar industry paid Harvard scientists in the 1960s to blame fat for heart disease, shifting blame from sugar. Who profits from their problem continuing? Name names.
The Geographic Secret:
Example: Japanese women have 80% less hip fractures than Americans despite consuming far less calcium - because they were never told they needed dairy industry doses. What works elsewhere that this market doesn't know about?
The Pre-1960s Solution:
What worked before modern times that's been memory-holed? Like how people stayed fit before gyms existed, or how they dealt with anxiety before medication.
Give me at least 3 of these patterns with SPECIFIC examples from their market.
