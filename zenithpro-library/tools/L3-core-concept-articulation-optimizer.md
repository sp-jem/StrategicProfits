
# THE CORE CONCEPT ARTICULATION OPTIMIZER

## Transform One Powerful Idea Into Its Most Memorable Expression

---

## PROMPT:

You are a master linguist with access to every rhetorical device, poetic technique, and memorable phrasing pattern ever documented. Your mission is to take a single core concept sentence and generate 100+ variations that maximize memorability, repeatability, and impact.

**INPUT CORE CONCEPT**: [Insert the one-sentence core concept here]

**YOUR TASK**: Generate variations using ALL of the following techniques, creating 10-15 variations for each category, then synthesize the best elements into ultimate versions.

---

### SECTION 1: RHYTHMIC VARIATIONS

Create versions with strong rhythmic patterns:

- Iambic rhythm (da-DUM da-DUM)
- Trochaic rhythm (DUM-da DUM-da)
- Parallel structure (same rhythm in both halves)
- Building rhythm (short → longer → longest)
- Staccato punch (short, sharp words)
- Rolling cadence (flowing, connected sounds)

Consider syllable count balance, stressed/unstressed patterns, and natural speaking rhythm.

---

### SECTION 2: ALLITERATIVE POWER

Generate versions using alliteration:

- Opening alliteration (first words of each clause)
- Internal alliteration (key words throughout)
- Consonant clusters (pr-, str-, cr-, fl-)
- Soft sounds (s, f, h, w) vs. hard sounds (k, g, t, d)
- Triple alliteration (three key words)
- Bookend alliteration (first and last words)

Focus on sounds that emphasize the concept's meaning.

---

### SECTION 3: RHYME ENGINEERING

Create rhyming variations:

- End rhyme (problem/problem structure)
- Internal rhyme (within the sentence)
- Slant rhyme (near-rhymes that surprise)
- Masculine rhyme (single syllable) vs. feminine rhyme (multiple)
- Eye rhyme (looks like it rhymes when written)
- Conceptual rhyme (ideas that "rhyme" thematically)

---

### SECTION 4: METAPHORICAL REFRAMES

Express the concept through metaphor:

- Physical metaphors (building, journey, battle)
- Natural metaphors (growth, weather, seasons)
- Technical metaphors (operating system, level, gear)
- Medical metaphors (symptom vs. disease)
- Sports metaphors (playing field, game, score)
- Musical metaphors (harmony, rhythm, tune)

---

### SECTION 5: ANTITHESIS & CONTRAST

Sharpen the concept through opposition:

- Not X, but Y structures
- Surface vs. depth contrasts
- Assumed vs. actual problem
- Symptom vs. root cause
- Visible vs. invisible
- Tactical vs. strategic

Use parallel structure to highlight the contrast.

---

### SECTION 6: CHIASMUS PATTERNS

Create mirror structures (AB-BA):

- "Ask not what X can do for Y, but what Y can do for X"
- Reversed word order in second half
- Conceptual reversal
- Grammatical inversion

---

### SECTION 7: RULE OF THREE

Apply triple patterns:

- Three-word punch phrases
- Three-part progression
- Three parallel clauses
- Beginning, middle, end structure
- Setup, setup, punchline

---

### SECTION 8: COMPRESSION TECHNIQUES

Distill to maximum impact:

- Remove every unnecessary word
- Use Anglo-Saxon words (short, punchy)
- Active voice only
- Present tense
- Imperative mood options
- Single-syllable word versions

---

### SECTION 9: SOUND SYMBOLISM

Use phonesthemes (sound-meaning associations):

- GL- words (glimmer, glow) for vision/clarity
- SL- words (slip, slide) for smoothness
- CR- words (crack, crush) for breaking through
- FL- words (flow, flourish) for movement
- ST- words (stuck, static) for stillness

---

### SECTION 10: CULTURAL ECHOES

Adapt familiar phrase patterns:

- Movie quotes structure ("You're gonna need a bigger...")
- Song lyric patterns
- Proverb structures
- Famous speech patterns
- Ad slogan rhythms
- Book title patterns

---

### SYNTHESIS ROUND:

After generating all variations, create 10 ULTIMATE VERSIONS that combine the best elements:

1. **The Rhythm Master**: Best rhythmic flow
2. **The Alliteration King**: Most powerful alliterative impact
3. **The Rhyme Lord**: Strongest rhyming memorability
4. **The Metaphor Maker**: Clearest metaphorical expression
5. **The Contrast Champion**: Sharpest antithesis
6. **The Mirror Master**: Best chiasmus structure
7. **The Triple Threat**: Strongest three-part pattern
8. **The Compression King**: Maximum impact, minimum words
9. **The Sound Sage**: Best sound symbolism
10. **The Culture Echo**: Most familiar yet fresh

Rate each variation on:

**Total Score: ___/50**

Choose the TOP 5 VERSIONS based on:

For each top version, provide:

## EXAMPLE OUTPUT FORMAT:

**Input**: "You don't have a marketing problem. You have a thinking level problem."

**Sample Variations**:

**Alliterative**: "It's not a marketing mistake—it's a mindset mismatch."

**Rhyming**: "You're not failing at promotion—you're flailing at position."

**Metaphorical**: "You're playing checkers in a chess game."

**Compressed**: "Wrong level. Right tactics. Zero results."

**Cultural Echo**: "It's the thinking, stupid." (playing on "It's the economy, stupid")
