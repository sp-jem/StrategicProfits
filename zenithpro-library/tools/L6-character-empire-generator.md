## INVISIBLE INFLUENCE INTEGRATION FRAMEWORKS

### Framework A: S.U.C. Quality Standard

Every character element must score 7+ on all three dimensions:

**STRONG (1-10):** Does this make a powerful, specific promise/impression?
- 1-3: Generic, forgettable, weak impact
- 4-6: Decent but not compelling
- 7-10: Powerful, specific, consequential

**UNIQUE (1-10):** Is this different from every other character in their space?
- 1-3: Same as competitors
- 4-6: Slight twist on common positioning
- 7-10: Never seen before, fresh, distinctive

**COMPELLING (1-10):** Does it create must-know curiosity?
- 1-3: "That's nice" response
- 4-6: "Interesting" response
- 7-10: "I NEED to know more" response

**Minimum threshold:** 7+ on ALL three. If any element scores <7, revise until it does.

### Framework B: Invisible Influence 5-Layer Design

For character installation to feel natural, design across all five layers:

**SURFACE LAYER (10%):** What they consciously experience
- The apparent self-discovery journey
- The "aha" moments they notice

**CONTEXT LAYER (25%):** Environmental framing that shapes interpretation
- Where/when character elements emerge
- What surrounds the revelation

**IDENTITY LAYER (30%):** Who they become by adopting this
- The identity shift triggered
- The "I am someone who..." statement

**BELIEF LAYER (25%):** The actual character belief being installed
- What they come to believe about themselves
- How this changes their self-concept

**DESIRE LAYER (15%):** Existing desire being channeled
- What they already want that this fulfills
- How character adoption satisfies existing needs

### Framework C: Micro-Commitment Ladder

Character adoption happens through progressive small agreements:

| Stage | Commitment | Identity Implication |
|-------|------------|---------------------|
| Recognition | "This resonates with me" | "I see myself in this" |
| Exploration | "I want to explore this" | "I might be this person" |

| Adoption | "This is working" | "I'm becoming this person" |

| Amplification | "I'm going deeper" | "I'm fully this person" |

### Framework D: Tension Design (70/30)

**TEACH (70%):** What's fully revealed about their character
**WITHHOLD (30%):** What creates hunger to embody more deeply

Strategic incompleteness creates pull toward full embodiment.

### Framework E: Polarization Architecture

**ATTRACTS:** [Who resonates with this character]

Adapt character installation based on:

**MOTIVATION:** How urgently do they want to embody this character?
