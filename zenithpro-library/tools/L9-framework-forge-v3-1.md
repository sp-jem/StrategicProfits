## THE INTEGRATED METHODOLOGY

### The Full-Spectrum Model

```
         ABSTRACT/CONCEPTUAL
                ↑
   LEFT BRAIN   |   RIGHT BRAIN
   (Logical)    |   (Creative)
   ────────────┼─────────────
     MODELS &   |   METAPHORS &
   CASE STUDIES |   STORIES
                ↓
        CONCRETE/SPECIFIC

Context Layer = Top half (Models + Metaphors)
Concept Layer = Middle (The Point)
Content Layer = Bottom half (Case Studies + Stories)
```

### The Three Layers

**LAYER 1: CONTEXT (Abstract - The Picture)**
- **Model** (left-brain): Visual diagram showing universal structure
- **Metaphor** (right-brain): Symbolic word picture that works across markets

**LAYER 2: CONCEPT (Middle - The Point)**
- **The Statement**: The universal principle/mechanism
- **The Word**: One-word summary that captures essence

**LAYER 3: CONTENT (Concrete - The Stuff)**
- **Case Studies** (left-brain): Multiple market examples with data
- **Stories** (right-brain): Human narratives from different contexts
